AI Meeting Assistant

MeetScribe turns a recorded meeting into an accurate transcript and a structured written record. Where a task owner or deadline was not stated in the recording, it is shown as Unspecified rather than guessed.

How to use MeetScribe

  1. Select or drop an English-language meeting recording above.
  2. Optionally list domain terms or acronyms that the recording uses.
  3. Click Process. The three stages run in order and you can watch their progress.
  4. Compare the raw and refined transcripts, review the minutes, decisions and tasks, then download the record as PDF, text or JSON.
